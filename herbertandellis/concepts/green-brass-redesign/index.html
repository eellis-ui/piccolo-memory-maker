How an engagement runs

  1. Pipeline audit

    A working session on your offer, ideal customer, sales process and current numbers. You leave with a written plan whether or not we work together.

  2. Build

    We map the market, build the target list, write the messaging and set up domains, inboxes, sequences and tracking. Typically two to three weeks.

  3. Launch and learn

    Outreach goes live across channels. We test angles, segments and timing, and double down on what gets replies.

  4. Book and hand over

    Qualified meetings land in your calendar with context notes. Everything syncs to your CRM so nothing leaks between us and your closers.

  5. Report and scale

    Weekly reporting on sends, replies, meetings and pipeline value. When a segment works, we scale it.
