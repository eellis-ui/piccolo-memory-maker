07 — Straight answers

Before you ask.

Who is this for?

B2B businesses with a clear offer and someone ready to take the meetings: agencies, SaaS, professional services, trade suppliers, and hospitality groups selling events and corporate bookings.

Do we need our own data or tools?

No. Data, sending and sequencing run on our own platforms. You bring the offer and the calendar.

Will it put our main email at risk?

Outreach sends from separate domains that we set up and warm up, so your main domain and day-to-day email stay protected.

What about GDPR?

B2B outreach relies on legitimate interest. Every message carries a clear opt-out, and anyone who says no goes on a suppression list. We walk through the detail in the audit.

Do you run the Google Ads and Meta accounts?

Yes. We set up the accounts, tracking, campaigns and creative, then manage and improve them every month. Ad spend is paid by you directly to Google and Meta.

How much ad budget do we need?

It depends on your market and your goal. We recommend a starting budget in the audit, based on what the campaigns need to gather enough data to optimise.

How is it priced?

We quote after the audit, once we know your market, volumes and channels. No guesswork, no padded packages.
