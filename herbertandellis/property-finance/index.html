What to have ready

Send these with your form.

Attach them as you go, or email them to hello@herbertandellis.com.

  • Development appraisalIncluding the core construction cost, contingency and CIL.
  • Property portfolio scheduleFor each property you own: address, current value, outstanding debt, monthly rent and lender.
  • Development experience / CVFor each past project: address, GDV, build costs, year undertaken and a short overview of the scheme.
  • Photos and reason for refinanceOnly for part-complete schemes: current photos of the site and why you are refinancing it.
